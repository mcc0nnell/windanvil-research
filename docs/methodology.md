# Research methodology (draft v0)

The project is exploratory. No vulnerability, deployment, affected release, or downstream exposure is established by the contents of this repository.

## Evidence gates

1. Identity: verify the canonical component, its accessibility purpose, source, and collection time.
2. Consumer: verify a direct or transitive downstream relationship with reproducible build or package evidence.
3. Version: identify the actual release, affected-version evidence, and available deployment documentation.
4. Reachability: test the actual relevant input path, privileges, or attack boundary if authorized.
5. Consequence: identify which essential accessibility function might be affected, and any equivalent fallback.
6. Intervention: state the authorized next step, testable completion criteria, and remaining uncertainty.

Unknown evidence remains unknown. A visualized edge cannot establish a security vulnerability. Accessibility consequence and technical exploitability are separate dimensions.

## Proposed 13-week research

Public discovery and method calibration first; then source-backed inventory and prioritization, followed by 24 bounded security briefs and up to three optional authorized technical cases. Finish with accessible Markdown/HTML, reproducible CSV/JSON, limitations, and costed next-step options. If fewer than 200 projects qualify, document the shortfall.

We do not assert participation by any maintainer or organization. No unauthorized scanning, exploitation of real users, or use of nonpublic government materials. Substantive maintainer outreach follows funding and appropriate support. Handle sensitive evidence under coordinated disclosure.
